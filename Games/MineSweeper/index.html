Mine Sweeper

Rules:

  • Click a cell to reveal its contents.
  • A cell can contain one of the followings:
    • Empty Cell
    • A Digit
    • A Mine
  • Avoid the mines at all cost.
  • An empty cell shows that all of its neighbours are safe.
  • A number annouces the amount of Mines among its neighbours.
